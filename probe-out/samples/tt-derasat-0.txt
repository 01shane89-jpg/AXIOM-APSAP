									<p>&#8220;Bahraini-Saudi relations serve as an exemplary model for ties between brotherly nations,&#8221; &#8220;these stances embody a relationship between the two kingdoms that is deeply rooted in history,&#8221; and &#8220;the security and stability of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia are an integral part of the national security of the Kingdom of Bahrain.&#8221;</p>
<p>These are excerpts from statements made by His Majesty King Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa—may God protect and preserve him—during his visit to the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia on November 10, 2026. During this visit, His Majesty met with His Royal Highness Prince Mohammed bin Salman bin Abdulaziz Al Saud, Crown Prince and Prime Minister of the brotherly Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.</p>
<p>The significance of this visit lies in both its substance and its timing. In terms of substance, it should be viewed through three interconnected dimensions: the lessons of history, the realities of the present, and the prospects for the future.</p>
<p>Historically, the two nations are bound by deep-rooted ties spanning various eras—ties consistently defined by mutual support. This evolved into a framework for regional security, most notably through the establishment of the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) on May 25, 1981—an organization that has proven its resilience in the face of regional upheavals that undermined other cooperative ventures. Through the collective will of its member states—including the Kingdom of Bahrain and the brotherly Kingdom of Saudi Arabia—the GCC has successfully upheld the principle that the security of the Arab Gulf states is indivisible.
