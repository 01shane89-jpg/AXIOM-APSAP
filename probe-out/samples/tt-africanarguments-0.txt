										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<p style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>The Royal African Society Announces the Successful Conclusion of the 2026 Film Africa Festival</strong></p>
<p style="font-weight: 400;"><strong><em>London, United Kingdom,  9th November 2026</em></strong></p>
<p style="font-weight: 400;">The Royal African Society is delighted to announce the successful conclusion of its 125<strong><sup>th</sup> Anniversary edition of Film Africa Festival which took place from 23 October to 1 November.</strong> Now in its 13<sup>th</sup> year, Film Africa celebrated the richness, diversity, and creativity of African cinema and brought together audiences, filmmakers, industry professionals, and supporters for an inspiring celebration of African storytelling. The festival screened 38 films from 30 African countries, and hosted two symposia, across 11 venues in London, Sheffield, Bristol, and Plymouth.</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400;">The festival provided a vibrant platform for African filmmakers to share compelling stories, highlighted diverse perspectives, and engaged audiences through the power of film. This year’s Film Africa Festival provided a special focus on<strong> The Role of African Women in Cinema, and the Importance of Sustainable Finance, Intellectual Property and Digital Technology </strong>for the future of African Cinema.</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400;">We are deeply grateful to everyone who contributed to making this year&#8217;s festival a memorable success. We extend our heartfelt appreciation to all our members, guests, and members of the public who attended screenings, participated in discussions, engaged with filmmakers, and supported the festival throughout its programme. Your enthusiasm, curiosity, and commitment to African cinema were central to the festival&#8217;s success.</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400;">We are particularly appreciative of our 125<sup>th</sup> Anniversary sponsors:</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Platinum category:</strong> Afreximbank / CANEX and Nuvion</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Gold category:</strong> Africa Finance Corporation and Oando</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Bronze category:</strong> DLA Piper, Omenaa Foundation, Nigerian Exchange Group, Oakwood Green Africa, and Air Peace.</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400;">We also thank the filmmakers, directors, producers, our industry partners, venues, volunteers, and the wider creative community for their invaluable contributions and continued support.</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400;">Film Africa remains committed to celebrating African cinema, amplifying African voices, and creating opportunities for cultural exchange and meaningful dialogue through film. The 2026 festival has once again demonstrated the power of cinema to connect communities, inspire conversations, and deepen understanding of the many stories shaping the African continent and its diaspora.</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400;">We look forward to welcoming audiences back for future editions, and continuing to celebrate the extraordinary talent and creativity of African filmmakers. To join us and to partake in our future events, please see: <a href="https://royalafricansociety.org/events/">https://royalafricansociety.org/events/</a></p>
<p style="font-weight: 400;">For further partnership opportunities, kindly send us an email to: <a href="mailto:ras_director@soas.ac.uk">ras_director@soas.ac.uk</a>.</p>
