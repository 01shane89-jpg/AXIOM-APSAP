Following the arrest of the independent fact-checkers at Matsadaash in late September, Lina Attalah was subjected to a six-hour interrogation by Egyptian authorities on October 21. She was later charged with operating a news organization without a license, despite the fact that Mada Masr has made numerous attempts to obtain one. In addition to updating listeners with the latest news about their struggles, Attalah discussed Mada Masr&#x2019;s origins, how the political situation in Egypt has led to such severe repression of independent media and how regional and international supporters are working together to fight the ongoing prosecution of Mada Masr, Matsadaash and others.&#xa0;</p><p></p>
