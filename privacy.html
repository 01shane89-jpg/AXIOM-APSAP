3. What stays on your device

OSAP keeps your work in your browser's own storage (local storage and IndexedDB): map points and photos, drawn areas, routes, imported shapes, keyword watches, workspaces, medical, evacuation and comms plans, analyst notes, and settings. None of this is sent to us. It leaves your device only if you export it, share a link, or turn on one of the optional features in section 5. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

"Use my location" asks your browser for your position, uses it on your device to centre the map or pick your country, and does not send it to us. On-device AI summaries run in your browser, or on an AI server on your own network if you set one up, and the reports never leave your device.

5. Optional features that send data elsewhere

  • Sync my devices. If you pair devices, your saved work is encrypted on your device with a key made from your sync code and posted to public Nostr relays ([CURRENT RELAY LIST, e.g. nos.lol, relay.primal.net]). The relays and we cannot read it. They see an anonymous key, the encrypted data's size and timing, and your IP address. Photos are not synced.
  • Push to phone. Alerts go through ntfy.sh to a random channel name made on your device. Anyone who knows that name can read the alerts.
