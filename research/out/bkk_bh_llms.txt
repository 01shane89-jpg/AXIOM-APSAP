# Bangkok Hospital
> Bangkok Hospital (Headquarters, Soi Soonvijai, Bangkok) is the flagship tertiary-care hospital of the Bangkok Hospital Group under Bangkok Dusit Medical Services (BDMS) — Thailand's largest private healthcare network. Founded in 1972, the headquarters is a multi-hospital medical campus offering advanced, internationally accredited care across virtually every specialty, with dedicated services for Thai and international patients. The wider network operates 25+ hospitals nationwide.
The headquarters at Soi Soonvijai is the network's primary site and the main focus of this site (path `/bangkok`). It is not a single building but a co-located medical complex of specialty hospitals sharing one campus, so a patient can move between cardiac, cancer, neuro/orthopedic, and general tertiary care in one place.
Key facts:
- Founded: 1972; flagship headquarters at 2 Soi Soonvijai 7, New Petchburi Road, Huai Khwang, Bangkok 10310, Thailand
- Parent company: Bangkok Dusit Medical Services (BDMS), publicly traded (SET: BDMS) — Thailand's largest private hospital group
- Network: 25+ hospitals across Thailand under the Bangkok Hospital brand
- Contact: 1719 (24-hour hotline, local) or +66-2-310-3000
- Languages served: Thai, English, Japanese, Chinese, Arabic, German, Indonesian, Khmer, Burmese, Vietnamese, Russian, plus interpreters in 20+ languages via the International Service Center
What sets Bangkok Hospital apart:
- **Multi-specialty medical complex on one campus** — the headquarters co-locates the flagship Bangkok Hospital with Bangkok Heart Hospital, Wattanosoth Cancer Hospital, and Bangkok International Hospital (bone & brain / neuroscience & orthopedics).
- **Accreditation** — JCI (Joint Commission International) accredited for the 6th consecutive cycle, plus HA Thailand; several network hospitals (e.g. Pattaya, Phuket) are independently JCI-accredited as well. The headquarters holds JCI Clinical Care Program Certifications including Primary Stroke, Acute Coronary Syndrome, Lung Cancer, and Orthogeriatric programs.
- **International recognition** — named by Newsweek/Statista among the World's Best Hospitals in Thailand for three consecutive years (2023–2025), Thailand Top 5 in 2025, and a Best Specialized Hospital in Asia-Pacific (2024–2025).
- **Advanced technology** — Robotic Surgery Center (da Vinci Xi system), Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy Center, comprehensive stroke and cardiac programs, precision medicine, and a medical robotics hub.
- **Sports medicine** — BASEM (Bangkok Academy of Sports and Exercise Medicine) is a FIFA Medical Centre of Excellence, the first in Thailand and Southeast Asia.
- **International & expat care** — dedicated International Service Center with 20+ languages of interpretation, plus an Expat Care Center with case managers and internationally qualified physicians.
- **Nationwide reach** — 25+ hospitals across Thailand under one brand and shared clinical standards, so patients receive consistent care close to home and seamless referral to the Bangkok headquarters for complex cases.
