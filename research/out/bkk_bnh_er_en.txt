 24/7 Emergency Medical Care for Tourists
 Why Emergency Readiness Matters When Traveling?
 Traveling abroad is exciting, but unexpected health issues can quickly become stressful—especially if you don’t know where to find help. Whether it’s a sudden illness, accident, or minor injury, knowing where to go for trusted care in Bangkok is essential.
 BNH Hospital’s Emergency Services for Tourists provide immediate, reliable medical assistance with multilingual staff and advanced facilities designed to meet travelers’ needs. In a city full of world-class healthcare options, choosing a hospital that is both reputable and tourist-friendly can make all the difference. With dedicated emergency support available around the clock, you can travel confidently knowing expert care is always within reach in Thailand’s vibrant capital.
 Emergency Medical Care You Can Trust —Wherever Your Journey Takes You 
 Whether it’s a sudden illness, accident, or urgent health concern, BNH Hospital’s international team is ready 24/7 to care for travelers in Bangkok
 Call us 
 BNH Hospital Emergency Center – Always By Your Side
 Services offered:
 🚑 Ambulance Dispatch & Emergency Transport – Our ambulance service operates 24/7, ready to respond quickly to urgent medical emergencies anywhere in Bangkok. A skilled team of emergency physicians and nurses provides expert care during transport, ensuring your safe transfer back to our hospital.
 🏥 Comprehensive Emergency Room – Our emergency department is staffed by highly experienced doctors and nurses. We provide thorough, precise medical evaluation and treatment for all kinds of urgent health issues, ensuring you receive expert care without delay.
 🏨 Hotel Call Service – If visiting a hospital is inconvenient or difficult, our medical team can come directly to your hotel room. A skilled doctor or nurse will conduct examinations, provide treatment, and coordinate any necessary follow-up care right where you are staying, offering comfort and convenience.
 📱 Telemedicine Consultation – For non-critical situations or when you cannot visit a medical facility, our telemedicine service enables you to consult with doctors via secure video calls from wherever you are. This service provides quick medical advice, prescription guidance, and support without the need to travel.
 ✈️ Medical Evacuation / Air Ambulance : In critical cases requiring urgent transfer to another region or even overseas, our medical evacuation and air ambulance services provide safe, rapid, and well-coordinated transportation. Equipped with advanced life-support technology, our teams ensure continuous care throughout the journey.
 🛡️Travel and International Insurance Support: Navigating insurance claims can be stressful when you’re abroad. Our experienced insurance support team is readily available to assist you with travel insurance and international medical claims. We handle paperwork, liaise with insurers, and help you with the Government Official Payment (GOP) request process, streamlining your experience with clear communication and dedicated support.
 Why Choose BNH Hospital for Your Emergency Needs in Thailand?
 ✅ Located in the heart of Bangkok’s central district – easy to reach within minutes from major hotels, embassies, and tourist hubs.
 ✅ International standards of care backed by decades of experience serving expatriates and tourists.
 ✅ Multilingual support: English, Chinese, Japanese, Chinese, Vietnamese, Burmese, Bahasa, Arabic and more – so you never feel lost in translation.
 ✅ A trusted reputation: one of Bangkok’s oldest international hospitals with a long history of care for the expat and tourist community.
 ✅ Integrated care: from first aid to specialist follow-up, ensuring continuity of treatment.
 Frequently Asked Questions
 What should I do if I have a medical emergency in Bangkok? 
 👉-Contact our 24/7 Emergency Hotline at 02-632-1000 or WhatsApp community. We will provide immediate guidance and dispatch an ambulance if necessary to ensure you receive prompt assistance.
 Can the hospital send a doctor to my hotel? 
 👉Yes, you can contact our Hotel Call service at 02-632-1000. We will arrange for a qualified medical professional to come directly to your hotel if you are unable to travel.
 I don’t speak Thai. Will language be a problem? 
 👉 Not at BNH. Our medical and support teams speak multiple languages, including English, Japanese, Chinese, Vietnamese, Burmese, Bahasa and Arabic.
 Can BNH handle serious emergencies like accidents or heart issues? 
 👉 Absolutely. Our Emergency Center is equipped with ICU-level facilities, trauma care, and specialists available on call.
 Do you provide help for medical evacuation or insurance coordination? 
 👉 – Yes, our International Insurance Team coordinates directly with insurance companies to simplify your claims process. Additionally, we offer air ambulance services accompanied by a doctor or nurse escort team to ensure your safe transport back to your home country if needed.
