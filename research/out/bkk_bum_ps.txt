 Patient Services Overview
Our patients are served by over 900 nurses and over 1,200 doctors and dentists, covering 55 sub-specialties. The Red Cross gives Bumrungrad its top classification of "A" – defined as "a tertiary care center which should be able to provide quality care in all medical specialties including invasive cardiology, cardiac and neurosurgery."
The hospital features a 24-hour Emergency Room; 19 operating theatres; four types of intensive care units (adult, pediatric, cardiac and a Level III neo-natal ICU); and a rehabilitation center.
Surgical facilities include two cardiac catheterization labs, 19 operating theaters (two specifically set up for cardiac surgery), a surgical navigation system; plus endoscopy, arthroscopy, lithotripsy and interventional radiology capabilities.

 Medical Coordination Office
 Bumrungrad International welcomes overseas healthcare professionals and providers to refer patients to our hospital for medical sevices. Bumrungrad is recognized as a referral center for specialty care, and we have a medical coordination team available to assist in all aspects of case management.
In our Medical Coordination team, we have 7 doctors and 12 nurses coordinate scheduling of procedures, attendant family questions during treatment, and follow-up care planning.

 Visa Extension Service 
Our aim is to assist patients and their accompanying family members who are unable to return to their home country due to medical reasons. We provide support throughout the visa extension process to ensure continued treatment and a smooth stay in Thailand.
We coordinate directly with Immigration Bureau Sub-Div.2, Imm. Div.1(Bangkok Immigration Office) to prepare required documents and facilitate visa extension procedures for eligible patients and accompanying persons.
For assistance, please contact the Business Center to schedule an appointment with the immigration officer at least 10 days before your visa expiry date.
Please bring the original passport when making the appointment.
