 We Operate Around the Clock
 The Emergency Clinic at Samitivej Srinakarin Children’s Hospital provides care for children 24 hours a day by qualified pediatricians and surgeons. Our clinic is ready to treat children who suffer from serious acute illness, require surgery, have ingested poison, and/or have been in an accident. Our team of advisors consists of various sub-specialty pediatricians, pediatric surgeons, brain surgeons, heart surgeons, orthopedist, urologist, general surgeons, ophthalmologists, and anesthesiologists. There are observation rooms, treatment rooms, surgery operating room, and medication room inside the Emergency Clinic. We also have foreign language interpreters for Japanese, Arabic, and Chinese on the 2nd floor of the Samitivej Srinakarin Children’s Hospital.
 Samitivej has a team of emergency rescue based in Bangkok. Our team can attend to immediate emergency illness care & services in Thailand. We have a standby emergency service in the capital of Thailand since we operate around the clock. If you are looking for emergency medical rescue in Bangkok, ring us. We have emergency ambulance in Thailand ready for 24 hours.
