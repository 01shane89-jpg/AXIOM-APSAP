# Bumrungrad International Hospital
> Bumrungrad International Hospital is a multi-specialty quaternary care medical center in Bangkok, Thailand, providing complex clinical care for Thai and international patients. Founded in 1980, the headquarters operates as a centralized downtown medical campus where multi-disciplinary teams collaborate on patient care, serving patients from over 190 countries annually.
## About
- Founded: 1980; main campus at 33 Sukhumvit 3, Khlong Toei Nuea, Watthana, Bangkok 10110, Thailand
- Campus type: centralized downtown medical campus with co-located specialty facilities and clinics
- Medical staff: 1,200+ physicians and dentists, 900+ nurses, 4,800+ total employees
- Contact: +66 2 066 8888 (general enquiries), +66 2 011 5222 (emergency)
- Location: within walking distance of BTS Ploenchit and BTS Nana stations
- Languages: Thai and English core services, supported by an International Patient Center with medical coordinators and interpreters covering 30+ languages, including Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Russian, and regional Southeast Asian languages
## Accreditation & Quality
- Joint Commission International (JCI) accredited since 2002 - the first hospital in Southeast Asia to receive this accreditation
- HA Thailand (Hospital Accreditation)
- Global Healthcare Accreditation (GHA) for International Patient Services
- DNV GL certification for Managing Infection Risk
- Westgard Veritas certification for medical laboratory quality
## Medical Technology
- Robotic Surgery Center using the da Vinci XI system for urology, gynecology, and general surgery
- Mako SmartRobotics and Mazor X Stealth Edition for orthopedic and spine surgery
- Lunit INSIGHT AI-assisted analysis for chest X-ray and mammography
- Smart Personalized HealthMatch and Biological Age assessment for preventive screening
- Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) for genomics
- On-campus wellness and supportive care integration via Vitallife Wellness Center (preventive medicine) and Esperance Clinic (integrative cancer care)
## Patient Services & Logistics
- Direct billing agreements with contracted Thai and international insurance providers and Third-Party Administrators (TPAs)
- Medical visa documentation support and airport concierge services
- Remote care via the Bumrungrad Telehealth platform and Bumrungrad Anywhere application for virtual consultations and second opinions
- Complimentary shuttle services between the campus and selected transit points; valet and multi-level parking on campus
## Clinical Focus & Key Medical Centers
The campus operates 60+ specialized centers and clinics across core multi-disciplinary areas, including:
- **Cardiology & Cardiothoracic Surgery** - heart screenings, electrophysiology, open-heart surgery
- **Oncology (Horizon Cancer Center)** - chemotherapy, targeted therapy, radiation, multi-disciplinary tumor boards
- **Orthopedics & Spine Care** - robotic-assisted joint replacement, minimally invasive spine surgery (MISS)
- **Gastroenterology & Hepatology** - advanced endoscopy, colonoscopy, digestive disease treatment
- **Neurology & Neurosurgery** - stroke management, neuro-critical care, complex brain surgery
## Patient Operational Guidelines
- Appointment preparation: bring updated medical records, imaging discs, and current medications; fasting (8-12 hours) required for specific blood tests and metabolic screenings
- Insurance verification: present valid insurance cards/policies at registration to confirm coverage and secure Letters of Guarantee (LOG)
- Recommended to schedule appointments at least 48 hours in advance
